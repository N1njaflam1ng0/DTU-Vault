### Question 1
Jonathan, an engineer employed by a large wind turbine developer, believes that there are so many unknown-unknowns in the development of offshore wind turbines, that we should 'just do it': develop larger turbines, install them, and accept there will be many issues, and respond to problems as they emerge. Based on the class, is Jonathan's correct?

- [ ] Yes
- [x] No

_Feedback: We observe certainty whenever we know exactly what will happen in the future. I have a known causal chain, stable prices, reliable schedules etc. Therefore, given that I know my preferences, I can easily weigh the options with regard to how they fit my preferences and make informed decisions._

_Whenever we know, that there are uncertain variables regarding our decisions, we are dealing with uncertainty respectively risk (in case we can put numbers on these uncertain variables). We know that we do not have the full information or cannot fully predict the outcome of a decision, but we are aware of the possible scenarios and the area of risk/uncertainty. So we know, that there are unknowns to our decision equation._

_And finally, there is ignorance, when we are not even able to design possible events and scenarios where the outcomes of our decision might deviate from the plan. Although we usually can imagine that something is not going according to plan, it might be very hard to define the actual threats or events that may lead to this deviation from the plan. In that case we are ignorant of the possible – or as Mr. Rumsfeld put it, there are things we do not even know that we do not know them._

_Although it may seem like decisions in this last case, when there is ignorance of threats and risk events, are not possible, even then informed decisions and risk orientated projects are possible and that we need not stumble blindfolded towards the unknown._

### Question 5

According to slides and our discussion in class, why do we need to manage risks? Choose the CORRECT option.

- [ ] The goal of risk management is to react to risks, and to encourage reactive management.
- [ ] Risk management process exists to identify the largest number of risks related to the project.
- [ ] The risk management process should be executed and completed in the early phases of the project lifecycle.
- [ ] Managers appreciate the importance of risk management process, as it makes projects highly predictable.
- [x] None of the above.

_Feedback: The goal of risk management is to react to risks, and to encourage reactive management: Incorrect, the goal of risk management is exactly the opposite: to encourage proactive management, and identify and react to risks before they occur._

_Risk management is a systematic approach to risk reduction, its key objective and most difficult task is to identify all potential risks related to the project: Incorrect, risk management is indeed a systematic approach to risk reduce, however, identification of a large number of all potential risks is not the difficult part of it, but rather to identify relevant risks, and manage them._

_Risk management should be executed and completed early in the project lifecycle: INCORRECT, risk management should take place throughout the lifecycle. One of its key mistakes is exactly that it takes place in the beginning, but is not followed up throughout the project lifecycle._

_Managers appreciate the importance of risk management process, as it makes projects highly predictable: INCORRECT, risk management manages uncertainty, but it does not change the nature of the project; if you are managing a highly uncertain and unique 'first timer', risk management will help you manage uncertainty, make more informed decisions, and even partly reduce uncertainty by avoiding some of the risks, but it won't change the nature of the project making it predictable._

_Therefore: None of the above is the correct option._
